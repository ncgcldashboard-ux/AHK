The Problem: Volatility & Efficiency

The current regime recovers fixed capacity costs through variable per-unit rates. As consumption drops (solar adoption or economic slowdown), the per-unit rate must rise, driving more consumers off-grid (a “death spiral”).

Chart: Marginal cost (line) and system demand (bars).

The Solution: Subscription Model

1

Subscribe to Capacity (MDI)

Pay a fixed monthly subscription charge based on sanctioned load to secure grid access.

2

Consume Energy at the Margin

Consumption is charged at marginal cost (baseline Rs 18/kWh), decoupling volume from fixed costs.

Key Policy Note: 35% Utilization Baseline

The CSC rates are standardized such that a consumer with a 35% load factor pays approximately the same as the current average tariff. Consumption above 35% yields savings immediately (effective rate declines toward marginal cost).

Generation Mix Context

The marginal price is driven largely by the thermal stack (RLNG/Gas/Coal). Solar abundance during the day creates a “Sunshine” slot opportunity, while evening peaks engage expensive peakers.

Why Opt-In?

  • 1

    Consumer Choice

    High load factor consumers benefit immediately. Low usage can stay on legacy tariff.

  • 2

    Incremental Consumption

    Pricing energy at the margin incentivizes higher utilization, raising demand and production.
